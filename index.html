Everything you need to manage your CC

Made to help you manage your CC content efficiently.

folder_special

Sets & Organization

Group your CC into sets and nested sub-sets. Toggle entire creator collections or individual packages on and off with instant batch operations and no file-moving hassle.

filter_alt

CAS Filters

Automatic package inspection classifies your CC by CAS parts, Age groups (Toddler to Elder), Gender, Outfit categories, and Buy/Build objects.

speed

High-Speed Merging & Cache

PlumbobForge compresses and merges CC into unified dynamic bundles by default. Your game reads unified files directly, significantly slashing the bottlenecks and boot times.

tune

Configurations

Create and use multiple profiles for different save files or gameplay eras. Switch active configurations in one click with automatic synchronization to your Mods directory.

label

Tags & Categories

Add and edit custom user tags, re-categorize items on the fly. Instant live filtering and search.

keyboard_command_key

Quick Jump (Ctrl + K)

A spotlight search feature. Press Ctrl+K anywhere in the app to jump directly to any CC package, set, configuration, or tool in milliseconds.

Built-in Game Maintenance Tools

No need for separate programs. All that you might need is already here.

delete_sweep

Game Cache Cleaner

Clean bloated Sims 3 cache files with 1 click to prevent gameplay glitches and texture caching corruption. Find out what needs or doesn't need cleaning.

shield

Conflict Scanner

Scans your library for conflicting packages. Highlights potential crash sources and conflicts before you start the game.

build_circle

Pattern Material Fixer

Detects and repairs material strings in custom patterns. Eliminates the infinite freeze in Create-a-Style.

category

Collection Creator

Create and customize in-game Buy/Build collections directly from your CC sets. Assign custom category icons and optionally hide objects from the general catalogs.

Already using CC Magic or S3MO?

PlumbobForge has a 1-click migration tool in the settings that can copy over your existing CC Magic or Sims 3 Mod Organizer setups. Preserving all your sets, configurations, and packages with no manual work required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, 100%! PlumbobForge operates directly on standard package files inside your Documents directory (Documents/Electronic Arts/The Sims 3/Mods). It is completely compatible with patch 1.67, 1.69, EA App, Steam, and original physical disc installations.
The 32-bit Sims 3 engine opens an OS file handle for every individual .package file. When you have thousands of loose CC files, disk I/O bottlenecks and file handle limits cause severe stutter and multi-minute loading freezes. Merging hundreds of items into unified bundles reduces operating system overhead by up to 90%, letting the engine read data in bulk.
No un-merging is needed! PlumbobForge uses a non-destructive architecture: all your original packages remain safely preserved in your central Library folder. You can easily click "Show in Folder", retag, or move any original CC item at any time.
